About Us

Empowering women with clarity, confidence, and cycle awareness.

Our Mission

The Ovulation & Cycle Tracker was created to help women understand their bodies in a simple, modern, and supportive way. Our goal is to make cycle tracking accessible, accurate, and beautifully designed.

Why We Built This

Many women struggle with irregular cycles, fertility questions, or simply want to understand their monthly patterns better. This app provides clear predictions, helpful insights, and a soft, calming experience.

Our Values

  • Accuracy and clarity
  • Privacy and safety
  • Beautiful, feminine design
  • Supportive and educational content

Designed with care to help you understand your cycle.
