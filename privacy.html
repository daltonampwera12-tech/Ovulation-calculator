Privacy Policy

This Ovulation & Cycle Tracker is designed to respect your privacy. We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifying information such as your name, email, or contact details.

Local Data Storage

Cycle information (such as cycle length, period length, and daily notes) may be saved locally in your browser to improve your experience. This data stays on your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser storage.

Third‑Party Services

This app uses Google AdSense to display ads. Google may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver personalized or non‑personalized ads. Please refer to Google’s official privacy and advertising policies for more details.

No Medical Data Collection

This app does not collect medical records or transmit any health data to external servers. All calculations happen directly on your device.

Your Control

You may clear all locally stored data at any time by clearing your browser’s site storage or cache.
