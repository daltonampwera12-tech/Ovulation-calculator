What Causes Irregular Cycles?

Understanding why your cycle may shift from month to month.

Many women experience irregular cycles at some point in their lives. A “normal” cycle is typically 21–35 days, but several factors can cause your period to come earlier, later, or skip entirely.

1. Hormonal Imbalance

Hormones like estrogen and progesterone control your menstrual cycle. When they fluctuate, your cycle may become irregular.

2. Stress

High stress levels can delay ovulation or stop it temporarily, leading to late or missed periods.

3. Lifestyle Changes

Sudden weight loss, intense exercise, or poor sleep can affect your cycle rhythm.

4. Birth Control

Starting, stopping, or switching birth control can cause temporary irregularity.

5. Medical Conditions

Conditions like PCOS, thyroid disorders, or high prolactin levels can affect cycle length.
