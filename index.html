Understanding Your Cycle

What is ovulation?

Ovulation is when your ovary releases an egg. It usually happens once per cycle and marks your most fertile time.

What is the fertile window?

Your fertile window includes the five days before ovulation and the day after. These are the days when pregnancy is most likely.
