Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to help you understand your cycle better.

How accurate is the ovulation prediction?

Predictions are based on average cycle patterns. Every body is unique, so results may vary slightly. Tracking regularly improves accuracy.

Can I use this app for fertility planning?

Yes — the fertile window and ovulation day help you understand your most fertile days. However, it should not replace medical advice.

Does the app save my data?

Your cycle information and notes are stored locally on your device only. We do not collect or store personal data.

Is the app free?

Yes — the app is completely free to use. Ads may appear to support development.

Can I install the app?

Yes — tap the “Install App” button on the homepage to add it to your device like a real app.
